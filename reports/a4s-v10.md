## Delivered

- `skills/roadmap/references/loop.md` ("Autonomous loop mode"):
  - The loop chains tasks without asking until one of 7 enumerated stop conditions applies:
    1. no executable task;
    2. Doctor required;
    3. a control is unknown or failed;
    4. the claim or lease is lost;
    5. a check, review, or gate fails;
    6. a human gate from the effective config applies;
    7. an implementation stage hits an unrecoverable error outside task scope or that violates contract invariants.
  - After closing a task it re-reads the Bead and the graph, then continues in deterministic order.
  - A final SUMMARY format is defined: tasks, PRs, merges, evidence, reviews, gates, stop reason, next step, and cleanup offered.
  - Loop commits require the `Bead: <id>` trailer, plus `Delivery-Override` when an exception applies.
  - Delivery follows `delivery_gate` literally; a human gate is a stop condition.
